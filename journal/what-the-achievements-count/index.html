What the achievements count

Posted: November 24, 2026 17:00 KST

The achievements are counted from your progress, so they reflect what you have already done in the game. There are 16 of them, worth 455 Game Center points in total.

What is counted

The first cleared level is one achievement. Clearing levels has four steps: 10, 50, 100 and 200 cleared levels. Total stars across your levels have four steps as well: 30, 100, 300 and 500. A three-star finish on any single level is its own achievement. Perfect challenges have three steps: 5, 10 and 30 different levels passed. The daily challenge counts the different days you finished it: 7, 30 and 60.

When they are checked

An achievement is checked when you clear a level, pass a perfect challenge, finish the daily challenge, and right after you sign in to Game Center when the app starts. Progress you made before signing in still counts.

Where they are kept

An earned achievement is saved on the device first. If you are not signed in to Game Center or you are offline, it is reported at the next check. The daily count is kept per device and is not merged across devices. Game Center reporting works on iOS only. On Android, the achievements are saved and listed on the achievements screen inside the app.
