No ads in Bottled Marbles

Posted: November 19, 2026 14:15 KST

Until recently, our privacy policy mentioned optional ads: ads you could choose to watch for a reward. On November 15 we decided against them. Bottled Marbles will not show ads of any kind.

What we decided

There will be no ads in the app, including reward ads. Instead, the app will offer paid in-app purchases. We have not decided what they will include or what they will cost, so there is no price to share yet.

Where things stand today

In the version we are testing now, the app contains no advertising SDK. Hints and the extra bottle work without watching anything, within a small limit per round, and undo is free and unlimited. There are no in-app purchases in the app yet either.

How purchases will work

When purchases arrive, payment will go through the App Store or Google Play, under their terms. We will not receive your card details. Before that version ships, we will update the privacy policy first, and we will write about it here.

Where the policy lives

Privacy and terms for this game now live in one place on ipe.rest: [privacy policy](https://ipe.rest/legal/privacy/).
