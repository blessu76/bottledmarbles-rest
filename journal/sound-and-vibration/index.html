Sound and vibration settings

Posted: November 25, 2026 13:00 KST

The settings screen has two switches, Sound and Vibration. Both are on by default. Turning one off stops it everywhere in the app.

Volume

The sound volume slider goes from 0 to 100 percent in steps of 5. The default is 70 percent. When you let go of the slider, a short sound plays so you can hear the new level. If sound is off, the slider is greyed out but keeps its value, so it returns to the same level when you turn sound back on. A volume of 0 does not turn sound off; the switch does that.

The sounds

There are four sound effects: selecting a bottle, pouring, a move that is refused, and winning a level. The app generates them itself as simple sine tones, so no outside audio files are used.

Your phone's settings

On iPhone the sounds follow the silent switch, and they play alongside music or audio from other apps. On Android they use the media volume, so they do not depend on the ring volume or Do Not Disturb. Vibration uses the phone's own haptic feedback, and it only runs while the Vibration switch is on.
