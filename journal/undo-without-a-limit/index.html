Undo that never runs out

Posted: November 20, 2026 13:30 KST

Every move in Bottled Marbles goes into a history. Undo takes back the last move, one step at a time. It costs nothing, and you can use it as often as you like on any level.

Why there is no limit

We thought about it and decided undo should stay free and unlimited. Blocking it would frustrate players more than it would help anything, and a puzzle is easier to think through when you can step back.

What undo does to your score

Each move adds one to your move count. Undo takes one away. A level's record is the lowest move count you have reached on it, so a move you take back no longer counts against you.

Restart and multi-marble moves

Restart puts the level back to its first layout and sets the move count to zero. When the top marbles of a bottle are the same color, moving them is one move, even if several marbles go across at once, as far as the destination bottle has room.
