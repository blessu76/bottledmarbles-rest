The home screen has a Today's challenge button. It gives you one level a day.

## How the level is chosen

The level comes from your device's local calendar date, which is mapped to one of the levels you have unlocked. A new player only gets early levels, starting with level 1. Once the level is chosen for the day, it stays the same even if you unlock more levels. Two devices with the same date and the same level file get the same level.

## What is saved

Only the date on which you finished it. After midnight you can play again. If midnight passes while you are solving, the completion counts for the date you started.

## What it does not do

The daily challenge uses no server, and it has no ads, no rewards and no streak counter. Playing it does not change your cleared levels, your best records or your leaderboard score, and no stars are shown or saved for it.
