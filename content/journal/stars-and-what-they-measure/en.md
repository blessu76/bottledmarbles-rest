Each cleared level earns one, two or three stars. The stars compare the number of moves you used with the fewest moves a solver found for that level.

## The rule

Three stars: you finished in the fewest possible moves or fewer. Two stars: within a quarter more than the fewest, rounded up. For example, if the fewest is 8, up to 10 moves earns two stars. One star: you cleared the level in any number of moves. Undo lowers your move count, so a move you take back does not cost you a star.

## How stars are shown

Earned stars are filled and missing stars are outlines, so you can tell them apart by shape and not only by color. Screen readers say "n of 3 stars". The clear screen shows the stars next to a "fewest: n moves" number, and the level map shows them too.

## What stays the same

Only your best star count for each level is kept, and it never goes down when you replay. In the version we are testing, using a hint or the extra bottle does not change how stars are counted. That can still change before release.
