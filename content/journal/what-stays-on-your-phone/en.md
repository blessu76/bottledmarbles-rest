Bottled Marbles is a calm sorting puzzle, and we wanted the app around it to be just as quiet. So it works without an account and without a server of our own.

## No account, nothing to sign in to

There is no sign-up and no login. You open the app and play. Nobody has to give us an email address.

## Your progress lives on the device

The app remembers how far you got and the fewest moves you needed on each level. That record is saved on your device and is not sent to us.

## What the app does not collect

At the time of writing, the app has no analytics tool, no advertising SDK and no comments or user-generated content. Crash information is not sent to an outside service either. The app is for players aged 16 and over.

## What goes over the network

You do not need a connection to play. The release notes now ship inside the app, so it no longer reads a page from this website. On iPhone and iPad, if you are signed in to Game Center, your leaderboard scores go to Apple's Game Center, not to a server of ours. Apple's privacy policy covers them.

## No ads, and paid purchases later

There will be no ads in the app, including ads you watch for a reward. Instead, we plan to offer paid in-app purchases. What they include and what they cost has not been decided yet. Payments will go through the App Store or Google Play, and we will not receive your card details. Before a version with purchases ships, we will update the privacy policy first, and we will say so here.
