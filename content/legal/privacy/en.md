**In short:** the Bottled Marbles app collects no personal information, has no accounts, no analytics and no ads. This website has no analytics or cookies; it only remembers your language choice inside your own browser. We do not sell data.

Effective date: 2026-11-14 · Operator: Davy Kim (ipe Studio)

## 1. Information we collect

Bottled Marbles ("the App") is a mobile game operated by an individual developer, Davy Kim (ipe Studio). The App has no accounts or sign-in, and it does not collect or send to any server any personal information such as your name, email, contact details, location, device identifiers or advertising ID. Your game progress and settings are stored on your device only and are deleted from it when you delete the App.

The current version of the App contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs. The App does not send your game data to any server we run. Because progress is kept on your device only, it is not synced between devices.

If we later add optional ads (for example, ads you choose to watch for a reward) or any online feature that changes what is collected, we will update this policy before that version is released and describe what is collected, by whom and why.

## 2. The website

This website (bottledmarbles.ipe.rest) uses no analytics, no advertising and no tracking cookies. If you pick a language, your browser may keep that choice on your own device (local storage, key `ipe_lang`); it is never sent to us. The pages load no fonts or scripts from third-party sites. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub's server-log policies apply to requests made to it.

## 3. Third parties

The App collects no personal information. We do not sell your information and do not share it with anyone except where the law requires. The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play; information related to downloads is handled by each store under its own privacy policy.

## 4. Questions and your rights

To request access, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of your personal information, or to ask any question, write to ipe.haven@gmail.com. We keep email you send us only to answer it, and we can find and delete it on request.

## 5. Age

Bottled Marbles is for users aged 16 and over. We do not collect personal information from anyone under 16, and the App collects no personal information from any user.
